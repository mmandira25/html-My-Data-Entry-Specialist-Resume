My Journey and Interest in Computer Technology

I have always been fascinated by computers ever since I took my first Computer Class during my freshman year of high school. Throughout my education, I developed a strong interest in computer technology and enjoyed learning how computers can make everyday tasks easier, faster, and more efficient.

After graduating from college, I started working as an elementary school teacher, teaching Math and Science. However, my passion for computers and technology never faded. I have always wanted to pursue a career in the technology field, and over time, I developed a strong interest in Data Entry.

I am now determined to pursue a career as a Data Entry Specialist. I am particularly interested in developing my skills in data management, Microsoft Office applications, spreadsheet management, data organization, and accuracy. I am eager to apply my knowledge and skills in a professional work environment, gain practical experience, contribute to an organization's success, and continue growing as a Data Entry Specialist.
